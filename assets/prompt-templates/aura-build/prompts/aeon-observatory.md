## Description (from the source page)

A cinematic, scroll-driven landing page built around a single living WebGL particle entity — 15,000 GPU-animated points that morph continuously from a gyroscope into a star, planet, ocean, DNA helix, comet, radio transmission, and rotating Earth as you scroll through eight sections. Each transition is physically simulated with flow-noise displacement, not a hard cut, and every shape has its own idle behavior: a charging heartbeat, a rolling tide, a slow independent gimbal rotation, particles that flee your cursor. Built on Three.js with custom GLSL shaders, GSAP ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography, and Lenis for weighted smooth-scroll — plus UnrealBloom post-processing, constellation-line reveals, scramble-decode typography, and a synthesized ambient soundtrack. Ideal for museums, science exhibits, observatories, research institutes, or any brand that wants scrolling itself to feel like discovery.
