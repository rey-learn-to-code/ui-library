## Description (from the source page)

An atmospheric real-time 3D landing page where scrolling walks you through a floating Japanese night market, from golden hour on the water to nine the next morning. Six chapters carried by one continuous camera move, with the sky, water, fog and every paper lantern changing as the night passes.

Built entirely in three.js with no images, models or textures. Every plank, roof tile and lantern is generated in code, so the whole thing is one self-contained HTML file of 357 KB that runs from a double-click.

Visitors can scroll it, jump between chapters from a button at each stop, or navigate from the progress bar along the top, which is itself a gradient of the night's own sky. There's also a time-of-day dial, drag to look around, and a key that hides the interface completely.

Suited to food and drink, travel, hospitality or any brand that would rather tell a story than stack sections.
