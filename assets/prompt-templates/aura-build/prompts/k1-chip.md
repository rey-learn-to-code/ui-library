## Description (from the source page)

Kerf K1 is a scroll-driven launch page for a fictional 3 nm class processor. It opens on a probe-card loader, then the chip powers on as a hologram and turns real. From there you scroll through how it's made: the layout drawn in light, the mask printing the wafer, the package coming apart into its eight parts. Then a laser cuts the chip open and the camera zooms from the package down to a single transistor. The text is written by a laser on its own clock, and every moment has a subtle synthesized sound. It's built with Three.js, GSAP and Lenis, runs at 60 fps, and has a full reduced-motion version.
