## Description (from the source page)

A scroll-driven story that starts with a flick of a switch. Afterglow opens in total darkness — visitors restore the power themselves, and every chapter after that glows: a hero that lights up as you scroll, pinned cinematic scenes, animated counters, a velocity-reactive project index, and headline type that burns like real neon tube.

Built for anyone whose work deserves atmosphere: artisan studios, portfolios, agencies, photographers, night-life brands, or any product with a story to tell. Twelve fully choreographed sections with buttery smooth scrolling, magnetic buttons, and a custom cursor — swap the copy and images and the drama comes free.
